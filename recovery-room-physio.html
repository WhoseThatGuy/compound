Recovery Room Compound Clinic

Rehab that ends
on the gym floor.

Most physio finishes in a treatment room and leaves you to work out the rest on your own. This one finishes on a platform, with a bar in your hands and someone watching how you move.

Recovery Room's Compound Clinic is set up for people who train — lifters, runners, field athletes and anyone whose injury keeps flaring up when they go back to it.

It's a short walk from the University of Otago and Unipol, which is why a lot of the people using it are students fitting rehab between classes and training.

01

In the building

The clinic is on the gym floor at Gate J, Level 2A. No second trip across town between your session and your appointment.

02

Nine platforms to rehab on

Loading a knee or a shoulder back up needs equipment. It's through the door — racks, bars, sleds and 46m of turf.

$0

First ACC appointment

Your first one is free. ACC appointments after that are $15.

What They Treat

Mostly the injuries
training gives you.

Recovery Room lists the following for the Compound clinic. If yours isn't here, ask them — this is what the clinic is built around, not the limit of what they do.

  • Gym and lifting injuries
  • Shoulder pain from pressing and overhead work
  • Knee pain from squatting and jumping, and tendon pain
  • Hip and groin injuries
  • Back pain related to lifting
  • Achilles and calf injuries
  • Return-to-running programmes
  • Post-operative rehab — ACL, meniscus, shoulder, ankle
  • Strength testing and return-to-sport planning

The Physios

Who you'll see.

Three of Recovery Room's physiotherapists work out of the Compound clinic. Their full team and bios are on recoveryroom.co.nz.

TK

Tom Kibblewhite

Leads the Compound clinic. Works in return-to-sport rehab and gym-based loading, so he's the one most likely to hand you a barbell as part of the treatment.

MA

Mark Alexander

Musculoskeletal physio. Diagnosis-led, and big on you understanding what's actually wrong rather than just being told what to do about it.

RW

Ryan Woodhouse

Performance rehab and progressive loading — getting people from injured back to training at full intensity, with numbers attached.

Physio FAQ

The usual
questions.

Who runs the physio clinic inside Compound Gym?

Recovery Room Physiotherapy & Performance. Their Compound Clinic is one of several Recovery Room locations around Dunedin, and it sits inside the gym itself.

Where is the physio clinic at Compound Gym?

Inside Compound, at Gate J, Level 2A, Forsyth Barr Stadium, 130 Anzac Avenue, Dunedin. You reach it from the gym floor, so there is no need to leave the building for treatment.

What does an ACC appointment cost at the Compound clinic?

Your first ACC appointment at the Compound clinic is free. ACC appointments after that are $15.

What injuries does the Compound clinic treat?

Gym and lifting injuries, shoulder pain from pressing and overhead work, knee pain from squatting and jumping, tendon pain, hip and groin injuries, back pain related to lifting, Achilles and calf injuries, return-to-running programmes, post-operative rehabilitation including ACL, meniscus, shoulder and ankle, and strength testing and return-to-sport planning.

How do I book a physio appointment at Compound Gym?

Book through Recovery Room, not through Compound. You can book online on their site or phone 03 477 0996.
