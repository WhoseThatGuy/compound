Membership Questions

Before you join.

Do I need to already be fit or experienced to join?

No. Athletes of all levels train at Compound. If you're new to strength training, your programme and two PT sessions will help you get started.

What if I don't really know what I'm doing on the floor?

That's what the training programme and 2 PT sessions with every membership are for — you'll get shown around properly, not just handed a key-tag.

Can I pause my membership?

Yes. You can pause your membership from the app whenever you need to.

What if I need to cancel before my 12 months is up?

You can cancel an Annual membership early for a flat $99 fee.

What happens when my 12-month term ends?

Your membership automatically rolls over. After the first 12 months, you can cancel any time with 3 days' notice and no cancellation fee.

Is there a joining fee?

No. There are no joining fees on any membership, Annual or Flexi.

What's the difference between Annual and Flexi?

Annual is a 12-month membership at the lower weekly rate. Flexi has no fixed term and costs slightly more per week.
