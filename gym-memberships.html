Do I need to already be fit or experienced to join?

No. Compound's floor has first-time HYROX entrants training next to national-level athletes, at the same time, on the same equipment. You get a free training programme and 2 PT sessions with every plan, so you're never just handed a key-tag and left to work it out.

What if I don't really know what I'm doing on the floor?

That's what the free training programme and 2 PT sessions with every plan are for — you'll get shown around properly, not just handed a key-tag.

Can I pause my membership?

Yes — pause for as long as you need, whenever you need to, right from our app. No calls or admin required.

What if I need to cancel before my 12 months is up?

You can cancel early for a flat $99 fee.

What happens when my 12-month term ends?

Your membership automatically rolls over. After that first 12 months, you're free to cancel any time with just 3 days' notice — no fee.

Is there a joining fee?

No joining fees on any plan, Annual or Flexi.

What's the difference between Annual and Flexi?

Annual is a 12-month term at the lower weekly rate, with the pause and cancellation terms above. Flexi is week-to-week with no term — cancel any time, no fee, at a slightly higher weekly rate.
