Built for training.

650m² inside Forsyth Barr Stadium, with nine lifting platforms, 40m of indoor turf and dedicated areas for strength, conditioning and warm-up.

Here's what's on the floor.

The Floor, Zone By Zone

Five zones,
one training floor.

Zone 1 — Warm-up

Space to get moving before you train. Treadmills, Concept2 erg bikes, kettlebells and dumbbells, plus swiss balls, medicine balls, resistance bands, foam rollers and trigger-point balls for mobility work.

Zone 2 — Upper Body

Dual-stack cable stations, dumbbells and selectorised and plate-loaded machines for pressing, pulling and accessory work.

Zone 3 — Lower Body

Nine lifting platforms with Eleiko competition and specialty bars, Industrial Athletic bumper plates colour-coded and calibrated in kilograms, plus lower-body machines including a 45-degree leg press.

Zone 4 — Full Body

Kettlebells, functional training equipment and open space for movements that don't fit neatly into upper or lower body training.

Zone 5 — Conditioning

40m of indoor turf for sled pushes, sled pulls and sprint work, with Concept2 rowing ergs, air bikes and treadmills.

The Training Environment

Chalk, bare feet,
dropped bars.

What you can and can't do on the floor.

Can I drop the weights?

Yes. Deadlifts, cleans and anything else that's better dropped than babied down.

"I was able to do olympic lifting training. A lot of gyms don't allow you to drop weights as part of…" — Reno Paotonu, Google review

Can I use chalk?

Yes. Chalk is allowed.

Do I need shoes on?

No. Barefoot and socks-only training is fine.

Can I Olympic lift at Compound?

Yes. We have nine lifting platforms, Eleiko bars and bumper plates, and dropping weights is allowed.

Is there space for sleds and sprints?

Yes. There's 40m of indoor turf for sled pushes, sled pulls, sprints and conditioning work.

Can I train for HYROX at Compound?

Yes. Compound has 40m of indoor turf for sled pushes and pulls, plus Concept2 rowing ergs, air bikes, treadmills, free weights and space for conditioning work. Sammy Burke coaches HYROX and endurance athletes here.

Will I have to wait for a rack?

There are nine platforms and we have never seen all nine in use at once. Five separate zones spread the floor out rather than bottlenecking it in one place.
