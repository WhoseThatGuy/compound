About Compound

Thirty years in Dunedin.
One change of direction.

Compound has been part of Dunedin's fitness scene since the early '90s. For most of that time we were an everybody's gym. In 2024 we stopped trying to be one.

The floor, the equipment and the hours are now set up around people who train — first week or tenth year, whatever they're training for. That narrowed who we're for, and it made the place work properly for them.

We're locally owned. We buy locally, hire locally, and give back locally.

What Makes Us, Us

In that order.

01

The facility

650m² laid out in five zones, each with its own floor space — nine lifting platforms with Eleiko bars, 40m of indoor turf, a full machine and dumbbell range, and infrared saunas. Purpose-built for training, not converted into it.

02

The environment

Clean, well-kept, and staffed by people who train here themselves. Chalk is allowed, weights can be dropped, and nobody's watching to judge where you're starting from.

03

The community

We didn't set out to build a culture. We built a facility that attracts people who are serious about training — the culture came after, and it's the part members talk about most.
