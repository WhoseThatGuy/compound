About Compound

Thirty years in Dunedin.
One change of direction.

Compound has been part of Dunedin's fitness scene since the early '90s. In 2023, we decided to build the gym around the people who cared most about training.

That meant better equipment, more space to train properly, 24/7 access, and a floor built around strength, sport and conditioning.

The result is the Compound you see today — a high-performance gym for athletes at every level.

Today, Compound is built around athletes who care about their training. Some compete at a high level. Some play local sport. Some are adding strength work to their training for the first time.

Different levels, different sports, same approach to training.

Dunedin is home. We're locally owned. We buy locally, hire locally, and give back locally.
