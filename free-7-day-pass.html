Free 7-Day Pass FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Seven days, everything a member gets, $0. Memberships start at $16.95 a week with no joining fee — full pricing's on the memberships page.

I'm visiting Dunedin — can I use the pass?

The free 7-day pass is for people living in Dunedin. If you're in town short-term, message us and we'll sort out a drop-in instead.

Do I need to be fit or experienced to use it?

No. Nobody's checking. Plenty of people start here with no training background, and every membership includes a training programme and 2 free PT sessions if you'd like a guided start.

What does the pass include?

Everything members get: the full training floor, lifting platforms, turf, infrared saunas, and free parking — 24/7 for 7 days.

What happens after I submit the form?

You can book a 5-minute key-tag setup straight away — or just walk in during staffed hours (Mon–Fri, 6am–7pm) and we'll set you up on the spot.

Do I need to book each visit?

No. Once your key-tag is set up, come and go whenever you like for the full 7 days.

What happens when my 7 days end?

If you want to stay, we'll get you set up as a member. Plans start at $16.95 a week, no joining fee — see the memberships page for all of them.

Rather look around before you train? Book a gym tour — we'll show you the floor, no workout required.
