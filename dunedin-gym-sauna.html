The Sauna

How members
use it.

01

After training

Plenty of members head straight from the gym into the sauna. Train, sauna, shower, home. You can also come in just to use the sauna — you don't need to train first.

02

Infrared heat

Unlike a traditional sauna that heats the air around you, an infrared sauna uses infrared light to heat your body more directly. This allows it to operate at a lower air temperature than a traditional sauna.

03

Included, not upsold

Sauna access comes with every Compound membership. No extra fee, no separate recovery membership and no paying each time you use it.
